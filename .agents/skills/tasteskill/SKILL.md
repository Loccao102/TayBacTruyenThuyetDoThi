# TasteSkill — Anti-Slop Frontend Philosophy

TasteSkill eradicates cookie-cutter AI aesthetics (generic purple gradients, uninspired SaaS cards, template grids, and flat plastic components). It forces intentional design judgment rooted in authentic materials, editorial curation, and cultural depth.

## Core Rules & Axioms

### 1. "Read the Room" (Genre & Cultural Context)
- Never impose a generic modern SaaS aesthetic on historical, cultural, or physical storytelling subjects.
- For this Northwest Vietnam Heritage Notebook:
  - **Genre**: *High-end Museum Folio & Tactile Heritage Journal (Sổ tay di sản điền dã)*.
  - **Mood**: Serene, deeply respectful, poetic, tactile, warm, timeless.
  - **Inspirations**: Vietnamese Dó handmade paper (giấy Dó Yên Thái), H'Mông indigo and beeswax batik textiles, ancient temple lacquered wood, cinnabar block prints, mountain mists of Hoàng Liên Sơn.

### 2. Typographic Discipline & Rhythm
- Avoid default browser type scales. Pair a soulful, high-contrast serif for headings (*Playfair Display* / *Cormorant Garamond*) with a legible, modern humanist sans for Vietnamese diacritics (*Be Vietnam Pro*).
- Optical balance:
  - Large headings must have tight leading (`0.95` - `1.15`) and slight negative tracking (`-0.01em` to `-0.02em`).
  - Small labels, kickers, and timestamps must use uppercase tracking (`0.12em` to `0.25em`) with medium font weights.
  - Body copy must have generous line height (`1.75` - `1.9`) for relaxed, editorial reading.

### 3. Physical Skeuomorphism & Material Authenticity
- When rendering physical objects (e.g. a vintage leather journal):
  - Do not use a flat box with a border.
  - Render physical spine curves, center gutter shadows, stitched leather edging, brass corner brackets, textured deckle paper edges, and woven silk bookmark ribbons.
  - Ink and stamp marks must exhibit authentic bleed, slight angular imperfection (-6° to -8°), and hand-carved textures.

### 4. Visual Density & Color Harmony
- Avoid pure `#000000` or sterile `#ffffff`.
- Ground all surfaces in organic tones: ivory parchment (`#faf5eb`, `#f4ecdc`), deep espresso ink (`#382620`, `#573e34`), muted cinnabar red (`#a63527`), aged gold leaf (`#b58c49`), and highland sage (`#456d4e`).
- Soft pastel thematic backgrounds for categorized knowledge cards.
